An Android inventory management system built for Jas Motor Shop. Tracks stock in real time, scans parts in with a barcode reader, and forecasts demand on-device so the shop can reorder before it runs out — without sending any data off the phone.

Android · Java Firebase TensorFlow Lite ZXing MPAndroidChart

Forecasting runs on a lightweight linear regression model trained in Python and exported to TFLite — picked over a heavier model to keep inference fast and cheap on a shop's ordinary phone.

About

background

I'm an IT/CS student focused on systems analysis and mobile development — the part of software where you have to understand how a business actually runs before you write a line of code. Most of my coursework and side projects follow the same loop: map the process, design the system, then build something people who aren't developers can use.

Outside of that, I split my time between video editing and a visual novel project, which mostly means switching between Android Studio and DaVinci Resolve in the same afternoon.
